    Memory Journey

    Close your eyes.

    Remember the last time you touched the ocean.

    Feel the temperature of the water.

    The salt on your lips.

    The sound surrounding you.

    The way your body felt weightless.

    You are still connected.

    Find Water Nearby

    Even small bodies of water can help reconnect.

    If you can't find natural water:

    • A pool or hot tub
    • A fountain in a park
    • A long bath or shower
    • Standing in the rain
    • A bowl of salt water for your hands

    Journal to the Ocean

    Write to it like a letter to a lover, a prayer to the source.

    Visualization

    Find a comfortable position.

    Close your eyes.

    Imagine you are standing at the shore.

    The water is at your feet.

    Feel it rise, touch your ankles.

    Cool. Alive. Connected to everything.

    You are never truly separate from the water.

    It is in you. You came from it. You will return to it.
