About this instrument

"Third eye" is the English nickname for ājñā — the "command center" between the eyebrows in yogic subtle anatomy, cousin to the "single eye" of the Gospels, the tisra til of the Sants, and the "eye of the heart" of the Sufis. Across traditions the reports rhyme: when attention is gathered from the senses and rested at one inner point, perception reorganizes — steadier, brighter, more unified.

This app packages the four classical methods for that gathering — gazing (trataka), breath (pranayama), inner light visualization, and mantra — with a library of the most electric public-domain contemplative texts we know, delivered slowly enough to work on you.

No account, no network, no data collected. Everything runs in this page.

Companion instrument: TIANMU — a study companion to the Celestial Eye teaching of Zhuan Falun, Lecture Two, built around reading the original text — and its Illuminated Edition, a passage-by-passage gloss with figures.

This is a contemplative practice aid, not a medical or psychiatric tool, and it makes no supernatural claims on your behalf. Strong meditation practice can occasionally stir up difficult material; if that happens, ground yourself — food, walking, conversation — and consider working with a qualified teacher. If you have a psychotic-spectrum condition, consult your clinician before intensive meditation.
