# AJNA — Third Eye Practice

A self-contained, single-file web app for classical "third eye" (ājñā / single-eye / eye-of-the-heart) contemplative practice. No build step, no dependencies, no network calls, no data collection — open `index.html` in any modern browser.

## What's inside

**Four guided practices** (timed, step-by-step on-screen guidance):

- **Trataka** — candle gazing with an animated flame, transitioning to the inner after-image at the brow point (per the Gheranda Samhita)
- **Bhrumadhya Breath** — coherent breathing (6 breaths/min) with a visual pacer, directing attention to the brow point
- **The Inner Star** — graduated light visualization
- **OM** — mantra practice moving from voiced to silent chanting (per the Mandukya/Mundaka Upanishads)

**The Library of Lightning** — ten of the most potent public-domain contemplative texts, delivered in a slow-reveal reader (one verse at a time, paced for deep reading):

Mandukya Upanishad · Mundaka Upanishad ("the bow and the arrow") · the "single eye" thread of the Gospels · Patanjali's Yoga Sutras · Tao Te Ching · Rumi · The Cloud of Unknowing · William Blake · Meister Eckhart · Svetasvatara Upanishad

**Voice mode** (browser speech synthesis, fully local): a spoken guided journey — **The Opening** (~15 min, eyes closed, slow-paced narration with deliberate long silences, backed by a soft OM drone) — plus spoken guidance in all four classical practices, spoken verses in the slow-reveal reader, and spoken phase guidance in TIANMU's study sitting. Toggle with the `voice · on/off` buttons; no audio leaves the device.

**Sound engine** (Web Audio, generated live):

- 6 Hz theta binaural beat (carrier 210 Hz)
- 10 Hz alpha binaural beat (carrier 200 Hz)
- 136.1 Hz OM drone with harmonics
- Completion bell

## TIANMU — companion page (`tianmu.html`)

A second, standalone instrument: a study companion to the Celestial Eye (天目) teaching of *Zhuan Falun*, Lecture Two. Deliberately contains **no substitute for the text** (which is copyrighted, though distributed free at falundafa.org) — per the tradition's own frame, whatever is transmitted is in reading the original. The companion provides:

- An annotated map of the teaching in paraphrase: the brow-to-pineal passage (animated diagram), the Five Eyes ladder, and the surrounding principles (no pursuit, xinxing-first, seeing ≠ cultivation, etc.)
- A guided **study sitting**: 2-minute preparation → open-ended paced reading of the actual chapter (with quiet posture reminders every 12 minutes) → 5-minute closing sit

## Illuminated Edition (`illuminated.html`)

A passage-by-passage illuminated companion to the "About the Third Eye" section of *Zhuan Falun* Lecture Two (2003 translation). Eleven folios in manuscript style, each covering one movement of the section in order: a short verbatim anchor line (the first sentence of the paragraphs covered, for finding your place), an extended faithful paraphrase, a margin note, and — where a picture genuinely helps — an inline SVG figure (the brow-to-pineal passage, the camera argument, the five-visions ladder, the virtue→vital-essence economy, sub-passageways, the endless tunnel, worlds-within-scales, and pursuit-as-occlusion). The original text is deliberately **not** reproduced (it is copyrighted, distributed free at falundafa.org); every folio deep-links to the official translation, and the intended use is both windows side by side.

## Verbatim study copy (`verbatim.html`)

The same eleven-folio illuminated layout, but with the **complete verbatim text** of "About the Third Eye" (all 28 paragraphs, extracted from the official 2003 translation at falundafa.org) as the primary layer, with gloss and figures subordinate. The text is © Li Hongzhi, distributed free by the rights holder; this copy exists for personal study in a private repository and should not be republished — link others to falundafa.org instead.

## Usage

```
open third-eye/index.html
```

Headphones recommended for the binaural tracks (they require stereo separation to work).

## Safety

These are traditional contemplative exercises, not medical treatments. The app includes in-context guidance: practice seated, blink freely during gazing, skip pulsing audio if seizure-prone, and consult a clinician before intensive meditation if you have a psychotic-spectrum condition.

## Sources

All quoted texts are public domain (Müller, Legge, Woods, Nicholson-derived renderings, KJV, and modernized medieval sources), lightly condensed for the slow-reveal format.
