TIANMU

a study companion to the Celestial Eye teaching of Zhuan Falun, Lecture Two
Read this first. This page contains no substitute for the text. Within the tradition itself, whatever is transmitted is held to be in the reading of the book — a summary carries nothing. The original is distributed free of charge in dozens of languages at falundafa.org (Books → Zhuan Falun → Lecture Two, "The Issue of the Celestial Eye"). What this companion does is prepare the sitting, keep your attention structured while you read the actual chapter, and hold the space afterward. The text does the work.

The Passage 通道

What the chapter says the celestial eye is — in paraphrase, as a map, not the territory.

The chapter locates the tianmu not in the skin of the forehead but as a passage —
from slightly above and between the eyebrows, inward to the pineal body.

In paraphrase: the lecture teaches that the pineal body already has the structure of an eye — a lens-like front and light receptivity — which medicine calls vestigial and the cultivation schools call complete. Cultivation does not build a new eye; it opens a channel between the brow and this eye so that it sees directly, without the optic nerve — and therefore is not confined to seeing this one dimension. Near the point of opening, practitioners commonly report a tightening at the forehead, a sensation of the flesh piling toward a point, a drilling inward, then darkness with light in it — a field of essential qi gathers before the eye like a mirror in which images can turn and appear.

The Five Eyes 五眼

Vision is graded. Tap each level — read from the bottom up.

The chapter's own emphasis: the level at which the eye opens is determined by xinxing — mind-nature, moral character — not by technique, effort, or wanting. "Cultivation depends on oneself; gong depends on the master." Nothing on this page or any page can raise xinxing for you.

The Study Session 學法

The creative part — and the humble part. The session below does not transmit anything. It seats you, quiets you, and then steps aside while you read the actual chapter, keeping a still frame around the reading the way a lamp keeps a frame around a page.

How it runs

1. Preparation — two minutes of settling, spine tall, breath soft.
2. Reading — open Zhuan Falun, Lecture Two, in the official free text or a printed copy. Read at a walking pace, aloud or silently. The companion holds a quiet clock and reminds you, at long intervals, only of posture and continuity — it will not interrupt the text with its own words.
3. Sitting — five minutes with the book closed, attention resting where the reading left it.
